Privacy Policy

FinanceSync · Last updated 13 October 2026

1. Scope

This policy describes how FinanceSync ("the application") handles information, including information received from Google APIs. FinanceSync is a private, self-hosted tool operated by and for one household of two people: the person who registered the application and their spouse. It has no other users, no sign-up, and no public interface.

2. Information the application accesses

  • Google Drive. With the account owner's consent, the application is granted access to specific folders in the owners' own Google Drive — the folder holding the household's financial statements, and the folder where weekly reports are archived. It reads the statement files in those folders and writes report files and newly received statement attachments to them.
  • Email. With the owners' consent, the application reads statements and statement attachments from the owners' own mailboxes. The mailboxes belong to the same two people referenced above.
  • Financial data extracted from those statements. Account balances, transactions, holdings, interest and dividend credits, and similar figures belonging to the two owners.

3. How the information is used

The information is used for one purpose only: to produce the household's own financial reports — month-by-month income, expenses, investments and net worth. Extracted figures are assembled into spreadsheets and charts and delivered to the two owners by email and into their own Drive folder.

4. What the application does not do

  • It does not sell, rent, trade or publish any user data.
  • It does not share user data with advertisers, data brokers or any other third party for their own purposes.
  • It does not use user data for advertising, profiling, credit assessment or eligibility decisions.
  • It does not use user data to train or improve machine-learning models.
  • It does not allow humans other than the two owners to read the data. No support staff, contractors or reviewers have access to it.

5. Limited Use

FinanceSync's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Where data is stored and who can process it

  • The owners' own devices and Google Drive. Statement files, extracted data and generated reports live on the owner's own computer and in the owners' own Google Drive. The owners control that storage, can inspect it at any time, and can delete from it at will.
  • An AI service, for written commentary only. To produce the short written commentary in the weekly report, the application sends aggregated figures — monthly income totals, category subtotals and the anomalies flagged in them — to an AI model operated by OpenAI, and receives back the commentary text. No account numbers, no credentials and no document images are sent. The AI service is used only to describe figures that the application has already computed; it does not calculate them.
  • Nothing else. There is no analytics, no telemetry, no error-reporting service and no cloud database operated by the application.

7. Retention and deletion

Data is retained for as long as the owners want the historical reports, and no longer. Because everything is held on the owners' own computer and in their own Google Drive, the owners delete it directly whenever they choose. Access granted to the application can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately ends the application's ability to reach Google data. A deletion request for anything held elsewhere can be sent to the contact address below and will be actioned.

8. Security

Access to Google data uses OAuth 2.0, so the application never sees the owners' Google passwords. Credentials and tokens are stored on the owner's own computer with file permissions limited to the owner. Access is limited to the specific folders the owners shared, rather than the whole Drive, wherever the API scope allows it.

9. Children

FinanceSync is not directed at children and is not offered to anyone outside the two owners described in section 1.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it, and the updated policy appears at the same address.
