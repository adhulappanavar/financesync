FinanceSync

FinanceSync is a private, self-hosted personal finance tool. It gathers the financial statements that a household receives by email — bank and credit-card statements, broker contract notes, mutual-fund and depository statements, and bond interest credit notices — into a Google Drive folder the owner controls, turns them into structured data, and produces monthly income, expense and net-worth reports as a spreadsheet.

Who it is for

FinanceSync is not a public service. There is no sign-up, no user accounts and no third-party users. It runs on the owner's own computer, for a single household of two people, and it only ever reaches financial data belonging to those two people.

How it uses Google

FinanceSync connects to Google Drive through OAuth, using a client registered by the owner of the application. Access is granted only to the folder that holds the household's own financial statements. That access is used to read statement files out of the folder for processing on the owner's computer, and to upload statement attachments that arrive in the household's own email. Nothing outside those folders is read, and no Google user data is sold, published or shared with anyone.

Where the household has separately authorised it, FinanceSync also reads statement emails from the household's own mailboxes, using an Apps Script on the owner's own Google account. Those emails belong to the same two people and are handled in exactly the same way as the Drive folder above.

Reports

Each week FinanceSync produces a spreadsheet with the month-by-month income position for the household, charts, and a short written commentary. The report is emailed to the owners, archived to their own Google Drive folder, and kept on the owner's computer.
